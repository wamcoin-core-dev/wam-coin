One value in the xbridge conf is worth a sentence, since it is not the
default: `Confirmations=60`.

The cost of reversing a confirmation is set by hashrate, not by block timing.
WAM's network measured 225 kH/s over the last 120 blocks today, and it falls
by about half in the quiet hours of the night. A swap settled at a shallow
depth on a chain that size is exposed for minutes, and the depth is the only
lever that changes that. It is reviewed publicly against measured hashrate
and comes down when the hashrate justifies it.

The same reasoning runs the other way, and we publish that too: 3
confirmations between two people for a small amount, 20 for an ordinary one,
60 where value leaves the chain and stops being reversible. Nobody rents half
a network to reverse a payment worth ten coins, so the 60 is for the place
where reversing actually pays, not a number applied to everything.
