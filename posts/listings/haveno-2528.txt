One thing worth stating, since it is ours to state and not yours to guess.

The depth at which a WAM payment should be treated as settled is set by the
amount, not by the kind of payment: 3 confirmations between two people for a
small amount, 20 for an ordinary one, 60 where value leaves the chain and
stops being reversible. The cost of reversing a confirmation is set by
hashrate, not by block timing -- WAM measured 225 kH/s over the last 120
blocks today, which is rentable hardware, so depth is the only lever there
is. If there is a per-coin confirmation setting on your side, 60 is the
number we publish for it, and it is reviewed publicly against measured
hashrate rather than left to age.
