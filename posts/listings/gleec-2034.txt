One value I would rather flag now than have you find later: the PR sets
`required_confirmations` to 4, and the figure we publish for a platform is 60.

The reason is hashrate, not block time. Mainnet is seven days old. Measured
today it ran between 142 and 414 kH/s inside one 24-hour cycle -- it roughly
doubles between the quiet hours of the night and the busy ones of the
morning, and a hash rate that small is rentable. The cost of reversing a
confirmation is set by that, so 4 confirmations is about eight minutes of
exposure, and about four at the night low.

It is your platform and your call, and I am not asking you to change it --
only to have the number before you decide. We review it in public against
measured hashrate rather than leaving it to age, the last review and its
basis are in the repository, and it comes down as the network grows.

For ordinary wallet-to-wallet payments we publish 3, for the opposite reason:
nobody rents half a network to reverse a payment worth ten coins.

third electrum -- say the word and it is there.
