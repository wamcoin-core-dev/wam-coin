THE CONFIRMATION LADDER — copy it from here, never write it from memory
=======================================================================

Claude got this wrong in three separate drafts on 2026-09-20 and 2026-09-22,
the same way each time: it wrote "for ordinary payments we publish 3", when 3
is for a SMALL amount and 20 is the ordinary one. The founder caught it all
three times. So the text lives here once and gets copied.

The source of truth is docs/LISTING_PACKAGE.md section 9, which also carries
the date of the last review and the hashrate it was reviewed against. If the
two ever disagree, that document wins and this file is stale.

---------------------------------------------------------------- paste below

    3    between two people, a small amount
    20   an ordinary amount
    60   where value leaves the chain and stops being reversible
    100  above a few thousand WAM

---------------------------------------------------------------- paste above

THE TWO SENTENCES THAT GO WITH IT

The depth follows the amount, not the kind of payment. An attack has a fixed
cost -- renting more than half the network for that many blocks -- so it is
only worth mounting against something worth more than that.

The low rows exist for the reason opposite to the high one: nobody rents half
a network to reverse a payment worth ten coins, so asking a person to wait
buys them nothing and costs them the use of the chain.

WHAT NOT TO DO

Do not quote 60 on its own. A project that only ever argues for more depth is
protecting itself; one that says where less is correct has thought about it,
and that is the difference a reviewer reads.

Do not call 3 "ordinary". That is the mistake this file exists to stop.

Do not write the number without the window it was measured over. The hashrate
behind it swings by roughly a factor of two between the quietest hour of the
night and the busiest of the morning -- 142 to 414 kH/s inside one day on
2026-09-21 -- so quote the low end of the day, because that is the hour the
depths are set against.
