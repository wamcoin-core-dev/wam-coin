--------------------------------------------------------------- 1 of 2

*v0.1.10 is out, and the headline is one command.*

`wam-miner --solo -u YOUR_ADDRESS -t 4`

You can now mine WAM against your own node with no pool at all. No account,
no operator, nothing between you and the chain. It needs a running wamd with
`server=1` in `wam.conf` and nothing else.

Find a block and you keep the whole 47.50 WAM instead of a share of it. You
wait longer between them — that is the trade, and it is yours to make.

And before spending any electricity:

`wam-miner --check -u YOUR_ADDRESS`

That builds a real block from your node's template and asks the node whether
it is valid. It hashes nothing and answers in about a second. Two bugs in
this code were caught that way before a single hash was computed.

Linux, Windows and macOS. From this release every build starts a regtest node
and runs `--check` on all three: the miner builds a real block and the node
must accept it, or no release is published at all.

Instructions: wamcoin.org/mine

--------------------------------------------------------------- 2 of 2

*The source now lives in three places, at three different companies.*

```
https://gitlab.com/WAMCoin/wam-coin
https://github.com/wamcoin-core-dev/wam-coin
https://gitea.com/WAMCoin/wam-coin
```

Same repository, pushed to all three together. Clone whichever you prefer. If
one is unreachable, the other two are, and you do not need us online to tell
you which to use — that is the whole point of there being three.

In September this project had one, and for a day nobody outside could reach
the code, the downloads or the site, because they all lived behind the same
account. Git is distributed and we were using it as though it were not.

The downloads are on none of the three. They are at wamcoin.org/downloads/,
on hardware this project runs, and the signature is what makes them
checkable: it covers the files, never the place they are hosted.

The signed list of everything that is ours: wamcoin.org/CHANNELS.txt
