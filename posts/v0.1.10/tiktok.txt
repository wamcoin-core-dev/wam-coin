TIKTOK -- CAPTION AND WHAT IS ON SCREEN
=======================================

TikTok is not read, it is watched. The command has to be legible on screen
in the first three seconds; the caption is only what somebody quotes when
they share it. No command they cannot read is worth speaking aloud.

--------------------------------------------------------------- NOTES

No price, no market, no "investment" -- not in the caption, not on screen,
not spoken. This platform's audience is the one most likely to hear a
mining video as a money video, and the paper says this project does not
make value. See the rule: we do not say it twice, but we never say the
opposite once.

No hash rate and no height. The video stays up; the numbers do not.
